Gensokyo was at peace.

Reimu Hakurei, the Hakurei Shrine maiden, was peacefully sipping tea.
With no visitors as usual, there was nothing to do at the shrine.
For the past few days, her routine had consisted of sipping tea with an occasional glance at the Great Barrier, as if she'd just remembered to check on it.
A familiar figure appeared in the deserted shrine grounds. A black witch's hat, a wave of the hand... That was...

Reimu "Marisa... right? Yes, it's Marisa."
Marisa "Huh? Who else would I be?"
Reimu "Hmm, am I getting forgetful? Your name slipped my mind for a second."
Marisa "Maybe all that slacking off has slowed your brain down? Nothing's been happening lately. It's nice and peaceful."
Reimu "Of course it's better when nothing happens. Or would you rather have more trouble to deal with?"
Marisa "Nah, I'd rather not have my hands full either."
Reimu "See? Things are fine the way they are. I've been busy enough lately as it is."

A shrine maiden's place is at her shrine, not running errands for everyone. So many incidents had cropped up lately that
her main job and sideline had switched places, but now things were back the way they were supposed to be.
Of course, returning to her proper duties hadn't brought in any more visitors. As usual, there was no shrine work to be done.

Reimu "By the way, did you come here for something?"
Marisa "No particular reason. Just felt like some tea on the way home, so I stopped by."
Reimu "And you thought you could get some here?"
Marisa "Why else would I stop by? Looks like I was out of luck, though."
Reimu "Honestly... I'd have shown you the door if you'd expected snacks too. Here."
Marisa "Oh, thanks."

As she sipped the freshly poured tea, Marisa took out a book.
Reimu let out an exasperated sigh. One look at the old, unfamiliar grimoire
made it obvious what Marisa had been doing and where she'd just been.
Back at the library, an asthmatic witch was probably drinking black tea in a bad mood right about now.
After a while, Marisa stood up. Her teacup was empty.

Marisa "Sorry for the trouble."
Reimu "If you're sorry, leave a donation on your way out."
Marisa "Any chance you could put it on my tab?"

Marisa rose into the air on her well-worn broom. Perhaps expecting Reimu to call after her with another complaint,
she sped straight off toward the Forest of Magic. Her figure grew hazy.
By the time Reimu had rubbed her eyes, Marisa was out of sight. Slightly envious of her speed,
she went to clear away the teacups.
That was when she noticed the grimoire Marisa had left behind.

The next day, Reimu reluctantly made her way to the Scarlet Devil Mansion. With the book Marisa had left behind in one hand,
she pushed open the heavy door to the Great Library.

Patchouli "Oh, what a rare visitor. What brings a shrine maiden all the way here—"
Reimu "Marisa left this at the shrine. It's one of your books, right?"

Reimu held out the book with an air of indifference. Patchouli took it with a suspicious look
and placed it on top of a pile of books on the table.

Patchouli "...I don't suppose I need to thank a thief, do I?"
Reimu "Hold on. A thief? Me?"
Patchouli "Aren't you? This is certainly one of the library's books, and we don't lend them out.
Yet here you are, bringing it in from outside. What else am I supposed to call you?"
Reimu "How rude. I'm just a shrine maiden. I told you Marisa had left it behind, didn't I?"
Patchouli "Marisa? Let me think... Oh, the magician in black.
I do seem to remember seeing her in the library now and then, but..."

Something about that answer felt wrong. Reimu couldn't understand
why Patchouli sounded so vague about her.

Reimu "Besides, you know I don't read grimoires. If I stole anything, it'd be a prayer book."
Patchouli "...Oh, never mind. The book's back where it belongs."
Reimu "Honestly... maybe I should've just sold it to Kourindou."

Still grumbling, Reimu left the library. Maybe Marisa would be waiting when she got back to the shrine.
She was a little curious to see how Marisa would react to hearing that Reimu had returned the grimoire.

But Marisa never showed up that day. Or the next. Or the day after that.
Puzzled, Reimu went to the Forest of Magic to look for her, but found no sign of Marisa.
She couldn't even find her way to Marisa's house. It was like being in the corridors of Eientei, Reimu thought.
Yet she could sense no distortion at all. That only made it more unsettling.

Then Patchouli's reaction suddenly came back to her.
Had Patchouli been struggling to remember Marisa?
Telling herself that couldn't be right, Reimu tried to recall Marisa.
Only then did she realize that she, too, was beginning to forget her.


Reimu "I can't find Marisa... No, it's more than that. Hardly anyone even knows who 'Marisa' is anymore."

Over the next few days, Reimu made careful inquiries.
What she learned confirmed her suspicions.
Even Patchouli and Alice, who should have known Marisa well, could offer little more than, "Oh, yes... wasn't there someone like that?"
Often, all she got was, "Who's that?"

Reimu "Maybe it's because we've known each other so long. I can still just about remember her name and what she looks like."

Even those memories were growing hazier by the day.
But knowing that didn't mean she had the slightest idea what to do about it.

Reimu "...Well, I suppose I'll start by tracking her down."

Gensokyo was at peace.

For most youkai and most humans, it was.
But Reimu's Gensokyo was different. Something precious was about to disappear from her Gensokyo.
Her idle days were over. Once again, the shrine maiden took to the sky.
